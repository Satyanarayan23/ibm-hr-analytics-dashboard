
# IBM HR Analytics Dashboard

## What Are We Trying to Solve?

Organizations need to understand their workforce demographics, employee distribution, job roles, compensation, and business travel patterns.

This Power BI dashboard provides an overview of employee data, helping users explore workforce characteristics and understand how employees are distributed across different organizational categories.

## What the Dashboard Shows

### 1. Employee Age Analysis
Explores employee distribution across different age groups.

### 2. Gender Distribution
Presents the workforce distribution by gender.

### 3. Marital Status Analysis
Shows employee distribution based on marital status.

### 4. Hourly Rate Analysis
Explores hourly rate distribution across employees.

### 5. Job Role Analysis
Compares employee distribution across different job roles.

### 6. Department Analysis
Presents employee distribution across organizational departments.

### 7. Business Travel Analysis
Explores employee distribution based on business travel frequency.

### 8. Education Field Analysis
Shows employee distribution across different educational backgrounds.
